I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Applied Statistics at Konkuk University.

Before joining Konkuk University, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Harvard University, where I worked under the mentorship of [Dr. Francesca Dominici](https://hsph.harvard.edu/profile/francesca-dominici/) in the National Studies on Air Pollution and Health ([NSAPH](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/nsaph/)) group. I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics at the University of Florida, where I was mentored by [Dr. Joseph Antonelli](https://jantonelli111.github.io/).

My research broadly focuses on causal inference, with particular interests in methods for continuous treatments and treatment effect heterogeneity. I primarily apply these methods to problems in environmental health.

**Students interested in causal inference and in working with me are encouraged to contact me by email.**
